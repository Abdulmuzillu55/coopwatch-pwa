How it works

Three layers turn a cheap sensor into a farm-wide early-warning system.

Sense

A DHT22 sensor sits one metre above the floor at bird height and measures temperature and humidity every cycle.

Process and send

A NodeMCU ESP8266 validates each reading, shows it on a 16x2 LCD and uploads it over Wi-Fi to ThingSpeak every 20 seconds.

See and act

ThingSpeak stores the data and e-mails an alert when limits are crossed. This website draws the live gauges, history and alert log.

About the project

CoopWatch is the web component of the undergraduate project IoT Based Monitoring of Temperature and Humidity in Poultry Farms, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, University of Science and Technology, 2026. Student: Adeyemi Oluwaseun Michael. Supervisor: Dr. Abubakar Suleiman.

Demo mode shows simulated readings. Connect your own ThingSpeak channel through Data source. Readings are decision support only and do not replace regular physical inspection of the birds.
